How this collection works. The original shortlist contains up to ten picks per registry project type, with further discoveries added after those picks. Ranked for software access, interesting data and room to experiment. Smaller categories have fewer picks. Ranks are editorial judgments. Category labels follow the registry’s primary category.
“High access” means documented custom software, firmware or a useful programmable interface. It does not mean every component is open or that flashing arbitrary code is effortless. A certification record verifies a published open design; it does not establish present stock, product support or medical validation. Availability labels are conservative and are not live inventory.
Source: OSHWA certification records and linked project documentation. Photos come from the respective project’s own pages; a line drawing indicates that no usable project photo was retrieved. Saved picks stay in this browser.
