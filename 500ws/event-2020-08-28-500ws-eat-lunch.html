28 August 2020Networking

500WS Eat Lunch with Dr.  Irene Adrian Kalchauser

Dr. Irene Adrian-Kalchhauser is a newly appointed professor at the Center for Fish and Wildlife Health at the VetSuisse in Bern and a member of the Committee for Academic Promotion of Early Career Researchers and Gender Equality (KANG).Her research is centered on the inheritance of gene regulation in particular in fish, but also in other organisms.Professor Adrian-Kalchhauser discussed with us the transition from postdoc to professor and the mentoring she received. She told us how she prepared for the professorship during her postdoc and about the importance of networking and how she negotiated salaries and funding.

¨500WS eat lunch" is a series of networking events in which our members have the opportunity to meet women leaders in academia, industry, funding bodies and research institutions and discuss career strategies with them and get valuable advice and insights.

This page records the event as announced at the time. Contact the pod for current activities.
