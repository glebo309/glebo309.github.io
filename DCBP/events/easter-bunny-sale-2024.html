Easter bunny sale

Almost 100 chocolate bunnies were bought and distributed around the department.

About this event

The Coalition organised an Easter bunny sale in March 2024. Members bought almost 100 chocolate bunnies, which the team distributed around the department.

Distribution was scheduled for 26 and 27 March, for recipients within the DCBP.
