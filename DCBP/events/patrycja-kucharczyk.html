Patrycja Kucharczyk: research and entrepreneurship

A seminar with the Treeless co-founder, selected through a community vote.

About this event

Dr. Patrycja Kucharczyk, co-founder of Treeless, gave the joint Bernese Chemical Society and DCBP Coalition seminar. Selected through a community vote, the event brought a research and entrepreneurship perspective to the department.

The seminar formed part of the Coalition’s programme of encounters with people working across academic and industrial careers.

The programme included an apéro after the seminar.
