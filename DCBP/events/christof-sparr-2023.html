About the programme

Prof. Dr. Christof Sparr from the University of Basel was the speaker for “Catalyst control over higher-order stereogenicity”.

The PhD Coalition selected and organised the Bernese Chemical Society talk, with Yaser Balmohammadi serving as its BCG ambassador.
