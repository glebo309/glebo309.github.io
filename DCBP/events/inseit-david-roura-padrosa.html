inSEIT spin-out: transitioning from the laboratory to a startup

About this event

The Coalition hosted David Roura Padrosa, co-founder of inSEIT, for “From university to a deep tech spin-off”. The seminar explored the transition from the laboratory to a startup, with a local example of a company emerging from university research.

The talk programme included an apéro in the cafeteria afterwards.
