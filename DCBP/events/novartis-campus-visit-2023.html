About the programme

A visit to Novartis Campus in Basel for PhD students, Master’s students, and postdocs. The programme included a CHAD presentation, pilot plants and laboratories, and the Wonders of Medicine exhibition.

A networking lunch provided by Novartis was included in the programme.
