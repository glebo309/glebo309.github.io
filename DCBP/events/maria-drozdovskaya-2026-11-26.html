Career talk

How does stardust become a planet?

The chemistry of star and planet formation

With Prof. Dr. Maria N. Drozdovskaya

From interstellar molecules to a research career

How do molecules in star-forming regions become part of planets? Maria Drozdovskaya will introduce her research on the chemistry of star and planet formation and share the scientific journey that brought her to the DCBP.

The talk will also explore her career path: how she chose her scientific direction, the decisions and challenges along the way, and the lessons she would share with students and early-career researchers. It is an opportunity to get to know a new group leader and hear how a research career takes shape.

Talk, questions, and apéro

There will be time for questions and discussion, followed by an apéro. PhD students, postdocs, Master's students, researchers, and staff from across the department are welcome.

About the speaker

Maria N. Drozdovskaya is an Assistant Professor at the University of Bern and leads the DCBP's Molecular Astrophysics group. Her research examines the chemistry of star- and planet-forming environments and the origins of planetary building blocks. She holds an ERC Consolidator Grant for the project Planetary Systems from Interstellar Ingredients.
