Our association

Built by researchers at the DCBP

The DCBP Coalition brings together PhD students and postdocs at the Department of Chemistry, Biochemistry and Pharmaceutical Sciences, University of Bern.

Our board organises events and community activities, from career conversations to practical workshops and informal gatherings. Members can bring ideas to the monthly meeting and help shape the programme.
