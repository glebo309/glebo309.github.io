Collection runs automatically four times daily while my Mac is available. The page keeps its last saved figures between updates; data older than 24 hours is marked below the heading.

Views and clones accumulate from recorded daily counts, including the available 14 days at the first collection. Repeated checks update overlapping days without double-counting. Missing history from before collection or a long outage cannot be recovered.

* Unique visitors and cloners are each repository's latest available 14-day figures. They are not summed into lifetime unique people. Clone operations can include automation; none of these figures measures active users.
